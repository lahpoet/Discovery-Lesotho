WHY LESOTHO?

A small country with big experiences

Lesotho is surrounded by South Africa and is known for its high mountains, traditional culture and outdoor adventures.

Mountain scenery

See the Maloti Mountains, valleys, waterfalls and highland landscapes.

Basotho culture

Experience traditional villages, Basotho blankets, music, food and pony trekking.

Outdoor adventure

Enjoy hiking, sightseeing, skiing and other activities for adventurous travellers.
