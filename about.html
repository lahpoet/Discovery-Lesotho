A QUICK INTRODUCTION

A country shaped by mountains and culture

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa. Its high altitude gives it a landscape of valleys, cliffs, rivers and dramatic mountain ranges.

The Basotho people have a rich cultural identity. Visitors can learn about traditional clothing, music, food, villages and the famous Basotho pony.

  • Capital city: Maseru
  • Known for highland scenery
  • Basotho culture and heritage
  • Outdoor and adventure tourism
LEARN MORE

Simple facts about Lesotho

Lesotho is famous for its high elevation and mountainous terrain. Much of the country sits high above sea level.

Basotho culture includes traditional clothing, music, food, crafts, community traditions and a strong connection with the highland environment.

Popular tourism ideas include sightseeing, hiking, pony trekking, cultural experiences, photography and winter activities.
TRAVEL CHECKLIST

Before you visit

Plan your route

Mountain roads can take longer than expected. Give yourself enough time and check your route before travelling.

Pack for the weather

Highland weather can change quickly. Comfortable shoes and warm layers are useful for outdoor activities.

Respect local communities

Ask permission before taking close-up photographs of people and respect local customs and heritage sites.
